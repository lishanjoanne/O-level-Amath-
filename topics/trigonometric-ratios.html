Geometry and Trigonometry

Trigonometric Ratios

Special angle values, the CAST rule for general angles, and the key identities (negative angle, complementary angle, reciprocal ratios). See also Trigonometric Graphs.

Key Formulas

Reciprocal ratios
$$\begin{aligned} \csc\theta &= \frac{1}{\sin\theta} \\ \sec\theta &= \frac{1}{\cos\theta} \\ \cot\theta &= \frac{1}{\tan\theta} \end{aligned}$$
Valid when $\sin\theta \ne 0$, $\cos\theta \ne 0$, $\tan\theta \ne 0$ respectively.
Negative angle identities
$$\begin{aligned} \sin(-\theta) &= -\sin\theta \\ \cos(-\theta) &= \cos\theta \\ \tan(-\theta) &= -\tan\theta \end{aligned}$$
Complementary angle identities
$$\begin{aligned} \sin(90^\circ-\theta) &= \cos\theta \\ \cos(90^\circ-\theta) &= \sin\theta \\ \tan(90^\circ-\theta) &= \frac{1}{\tan\theta} \end{aligned}$$
General angle — the CAST rule
$$\begin{aligned} \theta&=\alpha &&(\text{Q1}) \\ \theta&=180^\circ-\alpha &&(\text{Q2}) \\ \theta&=180^\circ+\alpha &&(\text{Q3}) \\ \theta&=360^\circ-\alpha &&(\text{Q4}) \end{aligned}$$
$\alpha$ is the basic (acute) angle. The letter in each quadrant names which ratio is positive there.

Notes

Special angle values

Derived from two right triangles: a $45$-$45$-$90$ triangle (legs $1,1$, hypotenuse $\sqrt2$) and a $30$-$60$-$90$ triangle (sides $1,\sqrt3,2$).

$\theta$$30^\circ$ or $\frac{\pi}{6}$$45^\circ$ or $\frac{\pi}{4}$$60^\circ$ or $\frac{\pi}{3}$
$\sin\theta$$\frac{1}{2}$$\frac{\sqrt2}{2}$$\frac{\sqrt3}{2}$
$\cos\theta$$\frac{\sqrt3}{2}$$\frac{\sqrt2}{2}$$\frac{1}{2}$
$\tan\theta$$\frac{\sqrt3}{3}$$1$$\sqrt3$
Mnemonic: Going anticlockwise from quadrant 1 — All, Students, Take, Calculus — matches All positive, Sin positive, Tan positive, Cos positive.
Worked example

Evaluate $\sin 150^\circ$ without a calculator.

$150^\circ$ is in Q2, so it's $180^\circ - \alpha$ with basic angle $\alpha = 30^\circ$.

In Q2, sine is positive (the "S" quadrant), so:

$$\sin 150^\circ = \sin 30^\circ = \frac{1}{2}$$
